Best Makeup for Sensitive Skin

Sensitive skin needs makeup that's gentle and reliable. Here's what to look for and why.

Choose Gentle Formulas

Look for fragrance-free, hypoallergenic foundation and skincare that calm rather than irritate.

Long-Wear Without Irritation

A breathable, long-wear base reduces the need to reapply and keeps skin comfortable all day.

Patch Test First

Always test new products on a small area. Build your routine around what your skin tolerates.
