Jewelry Care Tips: Keep It Shining

Proper care keeps your jewelry shining for years. Here are simple tips every owner should know.

Store Separately

Keep pieces in a dry, soft-lined box to prevent scratches and tangling.

Clean Gently

Use a soft cloth and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals that strip gold plating.

Wear & Remove Smartly

Remove jewelry before swimming, showering, or heavy activity to protect it.
